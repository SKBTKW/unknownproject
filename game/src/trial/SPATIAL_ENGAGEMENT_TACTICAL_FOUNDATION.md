# Trial Spatial Engagement / Tactical Foundation

This foundation separates three spatial facts: the battle location, the enemy approach, and the human engagement origin.

The attack direction is derived from origin -> battle location. Enemy advance direction is derived from enemy approach -> battle location. Downstream causality consumes the projected relative engagement meaning rather than raw coordinates.

A flank position is only positioning. It does not imply an ambush or tactic success. Concealment, awareness, initiative, deployment and other conditions remain separate tactic requirements.

Human engagement origins are semantic capabilities. The foundation intentionally does not branch on facility IDs. Local adjacent cells are the default candidate scope; linked and future military-network resolvers are extension hooks.
